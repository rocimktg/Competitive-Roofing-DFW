Privacy Policy

How We Handle Your Information

Straightforward, in plain language — the same way we run the rest of the business.

Last updated: April 2026

Who We Are

Competitive Roofing & Construction ("CRC," "we," "us," or "our") operates competitiveroofingdfw.com (the "Site"). This policy explains what information we collect when you use the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to visitors and to anyone who submits a form requesting an estimate or inspection.

Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you submit a form on the Site — for example, the "Get a Free Estimate" form in the footer or the estimate modal. Depending on the form, this may include:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Property address (if provided)
  • Service requested and any message or project details you enter

We also collect standard technical information automatically when you browse the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, through basic analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit to:

  • Respond to your estimate request or inquiry
  • Schedule inspections and follow up on active projects
  • Communicate with you about a quote, job, or service you've requested
  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Form Submissions & Spam Protection

Forms on this Site are processed through Netlify Forms. Submissions are stored by Netlify and delivered to us for follow-up. Our forms include a hidden honeypot field used to filter automated spam submissions — this field is invisible to real visitors and doesn't collect any personal information.

Cookies & Analytics

The Site may use basic analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand aggregate visitor behavior — which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive at the Site, and general engagement trends. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so won't affect your ability to use the Site or submit a form.

How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable precautions to protect the information you provide, including relying on Netlify's and our hosting provider's standard security practices for data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

If you'd like us to delete information you've submitted through a form, or if you have questions about what we have on file, contact us using the information below and we'll take care of it.

Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for homeowners and property owners and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as our practices or applicable law change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
