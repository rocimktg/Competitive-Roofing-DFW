Why We Started Competitive Roofing

Most of the roofing industry in DFW is built around volume — storm-chasing crews that show up after a hailstorm, knock on doors, and disappear once the job is signed. Craig built Competitive Roofing & Construction to work the opposite way.

Started Local, Stayed Local

Competitive Roofing & Construction has operated out of Coppell since 2010. We're not a franchise, and we're not a regional operation that expanded into DFW after a storm season. This is a local company that grew through word of mouth in the same neighborhoods it started in — which is still where most of our work comes from today.

Built Around One Person Being Accountable

A lot of roofing companies scale by hiring sales reps who work on commission — which means the person pitching you the job has a financial incentive to upsell, and no responsibility for how the work actually turns out. We built the company so that doesn't happen. Craig writes the estimates. Craig is on the job. If something's not right, you're talking to the person who can actually fix it.

Referrals, Not Door-Knocking

We don't run storm-chasing crews through neighborhoods after a hail event, and we don't cold-knock doors. The majority of our business comes from referrals — past customers, neighbors, and repeat clients. That only works if the work holds up and the process feels honest, so that's what we've optimized for since day one.

CRC vs. a Storm-Chasing Contractor

After a big storm, DFW homeowners get flooded with door-knockers offering "free inspections." Here's the difference between working with an established local company and a crew that showed up with the storm.

With Competitive Roofing

  • Local company, same address, since 2010
  • Owner writes and honors every estimate
  • No commissioned sales reps or inflated bids
  • Crew is still reachable next year, and the year after
  • References and reviews from actual DFW neighborhoods
  • Permits pulled and cities registered where required

With a Storm-Chasing Crew

  • Out-of-state company that follows hail tracks
  • Commissioned door-knocker writes the estimate
  • Pricing pressure to sign same-day
  • Company is gone once the storm season ends
  • No local reputation to verify
  • Permitting and cleanup are often an afterthought

Credentials That Actually Matter

Not every certification is meaningful — but these are the ones we think homeowners should actually check for.

A+ BBB Accredited

Competitive Roofing & Construction holds an A+ Better Business Bureau rating, reflecting our track record with customer complaints, transparency, and business practices over time.

Licensed & Insured in Texas

We carry the licensing and insurance coverage required to operate as a roofing and construction contractor in Texas — protecting you and your property throughout the project.

Owens Corning Materials

We primarily install Owens Corning shingles, manufactured at facilities in North Texas. That means shorter supply lines, consistent material availability, and a manufacturer warranty backed by a company with local roots.

Serving Coppell First, Then the Rest of DFW

Our core service area is Coppell and the surrounding cities where most of our referrals come from, but we take roofing and exterior projects across the DFW Metroplex.
