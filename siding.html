Siding Services We Offer

Whether it's a full re-side, a handful of storm-damaged panels, or trim work around a roofing project, we scope it honestly and handle it as a standalone job or alongside other exterior work.

Vinyl & Fiber Cement Installation

Full siding installation using vinyl or fiber cement, depending on your home, budget, and maintenance preference. We'll walk you through the real tradeoffs between materials instead of just pushing whichever is easiest for us to install.

Storm & Hail Damage Repair

Hail cracks and dents siding the same way it damages roofs and gutters. We inspect and repair storm-damaged panels, and can document damage for your insurance adjuster the same way we do for roof claims.

Panel & Board Replacement

Cracked, warped, or missing panels rarely mean the whole house needs new siding. We replace what's actually damaged and match the existing profile and color as closely as possible.

Trim, Fascia & Soffit Work

Siding, trim, and roofline components all work together to keep water out. When we're on-site for siding work, we check the trim and fascia underneath and address anything that's already been compromised.

Color & Style Refresh

Sometimes siding is structurally fine but visually dated. We handle full re-sides for homeowners looking to update curb appeal, with color and style options that fit DFW's climate and your HOA's requirements.

Vinyl or Fiber Cement? Here's the Honest Comparison.

Both are solid choices for DFW homes. The right one depends on your budget, your home's style, and how much maintenance you want to deal with down the road.

Vinyl siding makes sense when…

  • Budget is a primary factor in the decision
  • You want minimal ongoing maintenance
  • Your home's existing style fits vinyl well
  • You're matching an existing vinyl-sided home
  • Faster installation timeline matters

Fiber cement makes sense when…

  • You want maximum impact and fire resistance
  • Long-term durability matters more than upfront cost
  • Your home's style calls for a heavier, more textured look
  • You're in an area with stricter HOA material standards
  • You plan to stay in the home long-term

Siding Questions

How long does siding installation take?

Most full residential re-sides take a few days to a week depending on the size of the home and material. Panel repairs and partial replacements are usually completed in a single day.

Will hail damage to my siding be covered by insurance?

Often, yes, if it's part of the same storm event that damaged your roof — but it depends on your policy. We document siding damage the same way we document roof damage, with photos and a written scope, so you have what you need if you decide to file.

Can you match my existing siding if I only need a partial repair?

In most cases, yes, especially for common vinyl profiles and colors. Older or discontinued siding lines can be harder to match exactly — we'll tell you honestly how close a match will be before any work begins.

Do I need a permit for new siding?

It depends on your city and the scope of work. Most re-sides don't require a permit, but some cities do for larger projects, and HOA approval on color and material is a separate requirement in many DFW neighborhoods. We'll confirm what applies to your property.

Should I replace my siding at the same time as my roof?

If both are original to the house and showing their age, it can make sense to bundle the work — one crew, one mobilization, and less disruption overall. It's not required, though, and we won't push a bundled job if your siding is still in good shape.
